# Product Context

## Why
High-school students need efficient Algebra I mastery for courses and SAT/ACT/PSAT/CLT. Existing tools often separate “game” from “tutor.” Axiom Rising makes mastery the progression system.

## Problems solved
- Low engagement with traditional worksheets
- Skipping prerequisites without evidence of mastery
- Content duplication across state standards
- Weak explicit teaching sequences in many edtech products

## How it should work (Slice 0)
1. Player spawns on a training pad (third-person-ish).
2. Approaches an in-world terminal to start Lesson 1.
3. Explicit I do / We do / You do sequence with KaTeX, immediate feedback, mastery gate.
4. On pass: placeable blueprint pad/ramp + rank insignia + access to a second zone.
5. Progress persists locally; jurisdiction selector shows standards coverage view.

## UX goals
- Fortnite-familiar movement (sprint/jump, touch stick on mobile)
- Pedagogy invisible but non-negotiable
- Optimistic sci-fi academy tone (Red Rising / Fourth Wing / Hitchhiker wit)
- Always keyboard/text path for answers even when speech works
